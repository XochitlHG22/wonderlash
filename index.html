How to remove tubing mascara

One of the most loved things about tubing mascara is how easy it is to remove, completely without makeup remover.

  1. Soak the lashes with warm water above 38°C until the mascara starts to soften.
  2. Gently massage from root to tip with your fingertips and the tubes will slide off in whole pieces without mess.
  3. Rinse with warm water until everything is gone and gently dry with a soft towel.
